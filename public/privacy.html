Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 | App: Live Circular (com.jobcircular.app)

Welcome to Live Circular. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your data when you use our mobile application and website services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to job seekers in Bangladesh:

  • User Profile Data: When you set up your profile, you may optionally provide your name, phone number, educational qualification, district location, and preferred job category.
  • Profile Photo (Optional): If you choose to upload a profile photo, it is uploaded to our secure media server. Camera and photo gallery access are requested only when you initiate an upload.
  • App Activity & Bookmarks: Saved circulars, applied job tags, and notification read states are synced to secure cloud storage.
  • Support & Feedback: Messages, feedback ratings, and support inquiries sent directly to our team.

2. Data Collection & Usage Purpose

We use your data exclusively for:

  • Personalizing job circular recommendations based on your category and qualification.
  • Delivering push notifications about new job notices, admit cards, and exam results.
  • Synchronizing your saved circulars and profile across re-installs.
  • Improving application performance, accessibility, and user experience.

3. Push Notifications & Analytics

We utilize trusted third-party services to deliver push notifications and monitor app health:

  • OneSignal & Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used to deliver real-time job alerts and notifications. These services generate unique, non-personally identifiable device tokens to route notifications to your device. You can opt-out of push notifications at any time via App Settings or your device's System Notification Settings.
  • Supabase & Cloudflare: Used for secure cloud database hosting, API proxying, and offline caching.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties, marketing agencies, or advertisers. Data is shared only with cloud infrastructure providers (Supabase, Firebase, Cloudflare, Cloudinary) necessary for operating the service under strict confidentiality.

5. Non-Affiliation Disclaimer

Live Circular is an independent job information aggregation app. We gather public job notices from official government websites (such as bangladesh.gov.bd, bpsc.gov.bd, mopa.gov.bd, teletalk.com.bd) and reputed newspapers. Live Circular does NOT represent any government entity. All official government trademarks and logos belong to their respective owners.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is intended for job seekers and general audiences aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

7. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Your profile data and saved jobs are retained as long as you maintain your account in the app. You have full rights to request complete deletion of your profile data. You can clear local app cache at any time via Settings > Clear Cache, or request profile data deletion by contacting support at rudrodeb029@gmail.com.

8. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard HTTPS encryption (TLS) for all data in transit and restricted database row-level access controls for data at rest.
